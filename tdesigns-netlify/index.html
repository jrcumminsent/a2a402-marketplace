Project questions

Good details make better custom work.

Start with what you know. T-Designs will help work through the product, process, artwork, and next steps.

What can T-Designs customize?add

T-Designs offers embroidery, laser engraving, leather patches, UV-printed patches, custom apparel and headwear, signs, banners, gifts, and other personalized products. Send the idea if you are not sure which category fits.

Can I see a mockup before production?add

Yes. T-Designs confirms the design details before work begins. The online hat designer also lets you build a planning mockup for front, side, and back decoration.

Can I upload my own logo or artwork?add

Yes. Attach a PNG, JPG, WebP, SVG, or PDF with the quote request. T-Designs reviews the real file and confirms whether any artwork preparation is needed.

Which decoration method is right for my project?add

Embroidery gives a durable stitched finish, leather patches create a dimensional engraved look, UV-printed patches preserve fine detail and full color, and laser engraving creates crisp permanent detail on compatible products. T-Designs will help match the process to the idea.

What hat brands does T-Designs offer?add

T-Designs works with Outdoor Cap, Otto, Cambridge, Richardson, Legacy, Zapped, Anyrope, Durability, New Era, Pacific Headwear, and Nike. Exact models, colors, and availability are confirmed with the quote.

Why does custom work need a quote first?add

Custom pricing depends on the product, quantity, artwork, process, size, placement, availability, and timing. T-Designs confirms those details before sending a Square invoice or payment link.
